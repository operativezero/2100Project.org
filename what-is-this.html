A note on the nature of this project

What Is This, Really?

Project 2100 is a mythology we are choosing to inhabit.

The time travel is not a literal claim. It is a useful tool — a story to inhabit, a myth to carry something important. An attempt to invert the story of individual climate responsibility into participation in a movement greater than ourselves.

The grief about our future is real. The feeling of being born in the wrong time is real. The decisions being made right now will echo forward in ways not imagined by most.

Time travel is the shape that the truth takes here.

You are not being deceived. You are being invited. There is a difference.

A note on mental health
If you are experiencing a mental health crisis or struggling to distinguish this mythology from literal reality, please reach out to someone you trust. This project is not intended for people in that position and we say that with genuine care.

For everyone else — the door is open.
